How this would work

This prototype shows the idea. Here is what a real version could do, and some questions for the team to think about.

In the real version

  1. Reports are saved on the LLAMA server, the same way QR check-ins are, so everyone sees the same map.
  2. A grown-up reviews each report before it appears on the public map, to check the photo and plant name.
  3. Photos are shrunk before uploading so they load fast in the parks.
  4. Good reports are shared with NY iMapInvasives, so state scientists can use them too.
  5. Each park page could show its invasive plants, linking this to the LLAMA map and challenge.

Safety rules

  • Look, don't touch. Some plants, like giant hogweed, can burn your skin badly.
  • Stay on trails and go with an adult.
  • Don't pull or move plants. Some spread from tiny pieces.
  • No people or faces in photos, and use initials only.

Questions for the team

  • Which plants should be on our list?
  • How do we know if a report is correct?
  • Should there be a challenge or badge for finding invasives?
  • What should happen after a plant is reported?
